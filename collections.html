Silk Mark & Handloom Care

Preserving your heirloom weaves for generations to come.

All authentic Kanchipuram sarees at Saree Showroom carry the official Silk Mark India tag and Geographical Indication (GI) registry. Genuine Korvai sarees have an interlocked warp seam where body and border meet. In our pure zari weaves, silver thread is electroplated with 24K real gold, which burns cleanly to ash without synthetic melting.

Wrap your silk saree in pure breathable unbleached muslin or cotton cloth. Avoid plastic covers as trapped humidity can cause zari tarnishing. Change the fold every six months and air the saree in a mild morning shaded breeze without direct harsh sunlight.
