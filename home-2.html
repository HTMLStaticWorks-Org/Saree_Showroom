From Cocoon to Mandap

The 4-Step Journey of an Heirloom

How pure mulberry silk and electroplated silver-gold wire become an immortal wedding drape.

01

Sericulture & Warp

Grade-A mulberry silk threads are reeled, degummed, and spun into dense 3-ply twisted warps capable of bearing heavy pure zari wefts.

02

Silver & Gold Metallurgy

Authentic Kanchipuram zari uses a pure silver wire core electroplated with 24K real gold (tested 1.2% purity for enduring luster).

03

Triple-Shuttle Korvai

Two master weavers operate in tandem on a pit loom, intertwining contrasting borders and sacred temple spires with rhythmic shuttle precision.

04

Atelier Finishing

Every saree receives hand-rolled silk pico edging, cotton fall attachment, ceremonial tassel fringe, and Government Silk Mark certification.
