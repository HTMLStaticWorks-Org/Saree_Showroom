OUR HERITAGE

WOVEN IN TRADITION, DRAPED IN ELEGANCE

Discover the timeless beauty of Indian sarees with Saree Showroom. From the rich zari artistry of Kanchipuram silks to the elegance of Banarasi, Paithani and contemporary designer sarees, every drape celebrates India's rich textile heritage and the artistry of its weavers.

ESTABLISHED 1943
Four Generations of Trust
Since 1943

The Chronicle of an Unbroken Craft

Eighty-three years of safeguarding South India's pure silk handloom traditions.

1943

The First Pit Loom

Founded with two handlooms in Kanchipuram, dedicated to authentic temple weaves for local royal families and temple deities.

1968

Korvai Revival

Pioneered the artisan guild to preserve the sacred triple-shuttle Korvai interlock when machine power-looms threatened the handloom craft.

1992

24K Gold Zari Standard

Established the 100% pure silver-gold plating laboratory, certifying that every wedding saree burns cleanly to pure silver ash.

2026

The Modern Haute Horlogerie

Flagship salons in Chennai and Hyderabad offering private haute trousseau suites, worldwide video styling, and archival preservation.
