Anatomy of an Heirloom

Zari & Master Craftsmanship Decoded

Understanding the sacred motifs and ancient weaving metallurgy that distinguish authentic South Indian silks.

Temple Border (Gopuram)

The iconic triangular crenellations symbolize Dravidian temple architecture. In Korvai handlooms, each spire is interlocked with distinct warp shuttles to represent divine blessing and protection.

Mayil & Mayilkan (Peacock)

The royal vehicle of Lord Murugan. The sacred Mayil symbolizes regal poise and beauty, while the intricate 'Mayilkan' (peacock eye) weave locks miniature diamond reflections across the pallu.

Mango Buta (Paisley)

Representing fertility, abundance, and eternal life in Tamil wedding tradition. Our master weavers outline the manga with fine real-gold wire and fill the interior with micro-stars.
