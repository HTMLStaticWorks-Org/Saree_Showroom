SILK MARK INDIA • 24K GOLD ZARI
Origin: Kanchipuram, Tamil Nadu

Pure Kanchipuram Mulberry Silk

Known as the "Queen of Silks", Kanchipuram sarees are crafted from heavy double-warp and triple-ply mulberry silk yarns sourced from South India. The defining hallmark is the Korvai weaving technique, where body and border are woven separately and interlocked with a serrated temple seam.

Zari Purity: 1.2% Real Gold Electroplated
Weave Weight: Heavy (850g – 1,150g)
Ideal Occasion: Sacred Muhurtham, Weddings
Longevity: Heirloom (100+ Years)
Explore Kanchipuram Sarees Bridal Trousseau
HANDLOOM MARK • KADHWA WEAVE
Origin: Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh

Banarasi Katan Brocade Silk

Woven in the spiritual ghats of Varanasi, pure Katan silk is created by twisting multiple fine silk filaments together to form a firm, lustrous warp. Decorated using the intricate Kadhwa technique where each motif is hand-locked individually without loose floats on the reverse.

Zari Purity: Sonaroopa Gold & Silver Zari
Weave Weight: Medium to Heavy (700g – 950g)
Ideal Occasion: Sangeet, Receptions, Royalty
Motifs: Shikargah, Jaal, Floral Bel
Explore Banarasi Silks Request Video Viewing
FEATHER-LIGHT • FLUID DRAPE
Origin: Arani & Dharmavaram

Mulberry Lightweight Soft Silk

Engineered for modern celebrations where ease of movement is paramount. Mulberry Soft Silks undergo specialized yarn finishing that preserves the luminous sheen of pure silk while reducing stiffness, ensuring an effortless, fluid cascade on all silhouettes.

Zari Purity: Brushed Rose & Champagne Zari
Weave Weight: Ultra Light (450g – 600g)
Ideal Occasion: Day Weddings, Cocktails, Pujas
Drape Feeling: Buttery Soft & Breathable

100% ORGANIC COTTON • PIT LOOM
Origin: Chettinad, Karaikudi

Chettinad Pure Handloom Cotton

Handwoven by artisan families in the palatial heartland of Chettinad. Characterized by bold contrasting temple borders, geometric checks (kattam), and earthy vegetable dye tones. Supremely cooling under tropical climates.

Thread Count: 80s to 100s Combed Cotton
Weave Weight: Light (400g – 550g)
Ideal Occasion: Daily Grace, Office, Festivals
Wash Care: Gentle Hand Wash / Mild Iron
